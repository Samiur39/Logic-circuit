Logic circuit simulator

Type any Boolean expression and get its gate circuit. Switch to NAND-only or NOR-only to rebuild the same function from a single universal gate.

AND: · & * or just write AB   OR: + |   NOT: ! ~ or A’   XOR: ^   Words work too (AND, OR, NOT, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR) with spaces around them. Variables are single letters, constants are 0 and 1. Start with “Y =” to name an output. Enter several expressions, one per line or separated by ;, and they share inputs and gates.

Click an input switch to toggle it. Amber wires carry 1, grey wires carry 0. Each gate output is labelled with the expression on its wire (hover for the full text).
